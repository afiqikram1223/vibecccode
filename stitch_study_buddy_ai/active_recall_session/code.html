What is the primary function of the mitochondria in a eukaryotic cell?

Tap to reveal answer
Answer

The primary function of the mitochondria is to generate most of the chemical energy needed to power the cell's biochemical reactions, stored in the form of adenosine triphosphate (ATP). Often referred to as the "powerhouse of the cell."
